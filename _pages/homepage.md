I research environmental drivers of food selection and energy intake. My work combines sensory science and nutrition to understand how the properties of food affect food selection, energy intake, and risk for chronic disease. Much of this is centered around selection behaviors: what people choose, how much they take, and how those choices carry through to what they eat.

Another focus is identifying responsiveness to food properties. This helps to identify who is at greatest risk for overconsumption.

> **By measuring the sensory and environmental properties that increase how much we eat, we can redesign our environments to support healthier choices.**
